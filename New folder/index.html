Our Invitation

Come and See

We know many people are tired of performance-driven religion and disconnected church experiences.

What we have found is simple but powerful: people grow deeply when they live life together in authentic community centered around Jesus.

Our gatherings are relaxed, participatory, family-oriented, and centered around relationship with God and one another. No pressure. No performance. Just people learning to follow Jesus together.

Plan a Visit

Simple & Authentic

No stage lights, smoke machines, or professional performance. We gather simply in comfortable living rooms around coffee, Bibles, and real life.

Everyone Participates

You aren't just an audience member. Every voice matters, every person has a gift to share, and everyone helps shape our time together.

Relational Focus

We prioritize relationship over bureaucracy. We walk together through the highs and lows of life, acting as a genuine family.

Kingdom Life

Following Jesus isn't a Sunday-only event. We focus on abiding in Him daily, learning to live out His kingdom in Gwinnett County.

No Need To Be Nervous

What To Expect

It's completely normal to feel a bit hesitant when visiting a home church for the first time. We want you to feel comfortable and safe from the moment you walk through the door.

Sharing Meals Together

Almost every gathering starts with a shared meal. We break bread, laugh, tell stories, and connect as friends. You don't need to bring anything—just pull up a chair!

Open Discussion & Teaching

Instead of a lecture or sermon-performance, our teaching is conversational. We read Scripture together, ask honest questions, share insights, and discuss how to live it out.

Prayer & Encouragement

We take time to pray for one another's needs, encourage each other with structural sharing, and speak hope into life's difficult seasons in a highly supportive environment.
